JTech Forums Desktop 0.2.8 keeps replies in context and makes notifications easier to scan.

What's new:
- Reply in a compact bottom-anchored composer while keeping the topic visible. The reply editor is focused automatically so you can start typing immediately.
- Desktop notifications lead with the action, such as who replied, mentioned you, or liked your post, with the topic shown beneath it. Clicking a notification still opens the matching post in the app.
- Keep the Latest, New, Unread, Hot, Top, and Votes filters visible while account state loads. Selecting an account-only feed while signed out opens the in-app sign-in flow.
- Topic and private-message images preserve their aspect ratio and stay at a comfortable conversation size.
- Starting a new topic no longer revives an old submitted topic. A saved draft is identified and can be continued or cleared.

Installers:
- Windows: `JTechForums-0.2.8.msi`
- macOS: `JTechForums-1.2.8.dmg` (the package version follows the macOS bundle versioning rule)
- Debian/Ubuntu x86_64: `jtechforums_0.2.8_amd64.deb`

Windows may display a publisher warning because the MSI is not code-signed. macOS may ask you to approve opening an app downloaded from the internet. This is a community-built client, not an official JTech Forums organization release.
