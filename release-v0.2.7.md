JTech Forums Desktop 0.2.7 improves unread-feed access and keeps large post images at conversation scale.

What's new:
- The Latest, New, Unread, Hot, Top, and Votes filters stay visible while account state loads. Selecting an account-only feed while signed out opens the in-app sign-in flow.
- Topic and private-message images keep their aspect ratio and are limited to 520 x 400 dp, so large attachments no longer fill the entire conversation pane.

Installers:
- Windows: `JTechForums-0.2.7.msi`
- macOS: `JTechForums-1.2.7.dmg` (the package version follows the macOS bundle versioning rule)
- Debian/Ubuntu x86_64: `jtechforums_0.2.7_amd64.deb`

Windows may display a publisher warning because the MSI is not code-signed. macOS may ask you to approve opening an app downloaded from the internet. This is a community-built client, not an official JTech Forums organization release.
